HOW TO READ THIS SHEET
"Intrinsic resistance is expressed as WHO ATC codes. The 7-character column gives the specific drugs the organism is intrinsically resistant to (precise). The 5-character column gives the level 4 class of those drugs; a class may include other drugs that are still active (e.g. J01CA includes ampicillin, which Klebsiella resists, and piperacillin, which it does not), so use the 7-character column when precision matters."
"Source basis: CLSI M100 Appendix B and EUCAST Expected Resistant Phenotypes, summarized from memory, plus published literature where those are silent. Rows with Low confidence are shaded orange."

ATC CODE CAVEATS
"Imipenem is coded J01DH51 (imipenem + cilastatin). Cefoxitin is J01DC01. ATC codes are assigned to substances, not to resistance, so these are the codes of the drugs affected. Novobiocin is not included because I could not confirm an ATC code for it."
Please verify against the WHO ATC/DDD Index (atcddd.fhi.no) and current CLSI/EUCAST tables before clinical or regulatory use.
